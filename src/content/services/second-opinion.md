## ¿Para quién?

Parejas que quieren alinear sus finanzas, familias que desean organizar su presupuesto o grupos con metas financieras en común.

## ¿Qué lograrás?

Mejor comunicación sobre dinero, un plan financiero común y acciones concretas para alcanzar sus metas juntos.

## When you do not need a project

Sometimes the honest answer is that you do not need six weeks of design. You need someone
to look hard at one thing and tell you what is wrong with it.

This is that. One week, one flow, and a document at the end that says what is costing you
and in what order to fix it.

## Ranked by cost, not by ease

Findings get ordered by what they are costing you — in conversions, in support load, in
engineering time — rather than by how quick they would be to fix. Easy fixes get marked
as such, but they do not get promoted for being easy.

## What you get

A written verdict, findings ranked and costed, and a call at the end where you get to
argue with the conclusions. No slides.
