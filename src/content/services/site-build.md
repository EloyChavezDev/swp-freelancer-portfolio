## ¿Para quién?

Empresas que quieren fortalecer la cultura financiera de sus colaboradores, colegios, universidades o comunidades que buscan aprender finanzas personales.

## ¿Qué logramos?

Mejor toma de decisiones financieras, mayor bienestar económico y una cultura financiera más sólida para tu organización o comunidad.

## Getting edits out of the backlog

The most common problem with a marketing site is not how it looks. It is that changing a
sentence requires an engineer. Six months of that and the site stops saying anything
current, because nobody wants to spend a pull request on a headline.

The fix is a content model: a small set of blocks the marketing team assembles pages
from, with the content itself in files anyone can edit.

## Performance is a design decision

Fast sites are mostly the result of choices made early — how many fonts, how many
scripts, whether images are sized before they ship. Those get decided at the start rather
than fixed in a sprint afterwards.

## What you get

Designed and built templates, a documented content model, the components in your
repository, and a walkthrough with whoever will be publishing.
